About the Artist

Hello! I'm Owen Gillespie, the creator behind OTG Productions. I specialize in traditional painting, contemporary drawing, and fine art photography. I am currently a sophomore at Miami University studying Studio Art and Emerging Technology in Business Design.

Browse through my recent work below! Original artworks and prints are listed with prices. For merch, custom work, or inquiries, check out the Shop and Commission pages.
